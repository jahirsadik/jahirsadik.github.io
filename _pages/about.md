Hello, I'm <a href="https://www.name-coach.com/jahirsadik-monon" class="name-pronounce" title="How to pronounce my name">Jahir</a>, a second-year MS/PhD student in Computer Science at the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://www.umass.edu/robotics/), advised by [Dr. Shlomo Zilberstein](https://groups.cs.umass.edu/shlomo/) in the Resource-Bounded Reasoning Lab and co-advised by [Dr. Donghyun Kim](https://www.umass.edu/robotics/people/donghyun-kim) in the Dynamic and Autonomous Robotic Systems ([DARoS](https://www.umass.edu/robotics/daros)) Lab.

I study **how embodied agents can make good decisions under limited onboard computation**. My work spans VLA-based navigation for assistive robots — much of it part of our lab's [guide dog robot](https://www.umass.edu/robotics/daros/research/guide-dog-robot) project — and perception datasets with synthetic data pipelines for blind and low-vision travelers. I am now moving toward small VLA models and multi-agent collaboration among compact foundation models, building on my earlier work in decentralized multi-agent RL and representation learning.

Before UMass, I was a research assistant at [CCDS](https://ccds.ai/), Independent University Bangladesh, where I worked on VLM-based agent-centric representations for RL with [Dr. Riashat Islam](https://riashat.github.io/) (Mila, McGill University & Microsoft Research), and on multimodal reasoning and LLM-based multi-agent collaboration with [Dr. Amin Ahsan Ali](http://www.cse.iub.edu.bd/faculties/53) and [Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman](http://www.cse.iub.edu.bd/faculties/56) of CCDS, IUB and [Muntasir Wahed](https://mwahed.com/) of UIUC. I did my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at the [University of Dhaka](https://du.ac.bd/programDetails/CSE/396), graduating with the Dean's Honor Award; my thesis with [Dr. Md Mosaddek Khan](https://mmkhansajeeb.com/) was on decentralized multi-agent reinforcement learning.
